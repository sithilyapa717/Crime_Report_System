# Crime Report System – staff guide (draft)

**Draft in English for translation.** The final staff guide is in Sinhala (D12). Screen captions are written here as the app shows them; where a caption is still English in the app, the Sinhala wording is not approved yet.

## 1. Starting and logging in

1. Open **Excise Crime Reports** from the desktop or the Start menu.
2. Click the yellow button for your section: **අධික්ෂණ නිළධාරීන්** (supervising), **සුරාබදු ස්ථානාධිපති** (station head), **ග්‍රහණ නිළධාරීන්** (detecting officers) or **නිරික්ෂණ නිළධාරීන්** (observation officers).
3. Type **your own** user name and password. Each person has their own account; passwords are never shared.
4. The first time you log in, or after the station head resets your password, the app asks you to choose your own password first.

If the app says your account is locked (5 wrong passwords), ask the station head to unlock it.

**Change password:** at the top right. **Log out:** at the top right; always log out when you leave the PC.

## 2. The main menu

The buttons are the same as on the old main menu. You only see what your section may do:

| Section              | Can                                                             |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Station head         | Everything, including the lists (සංස්කරණය) and Administration   |
| Detecting officers   | Enter and edit CRs, case progress, searches, reports, documents |
| Observation officers | View CRs, searches, reports                                     |
| Supervising officers | View CRs, searches, reports, including per-officer reports      |

**Back** returns to the previous screen; **Main menu** goes home. If you leave a screen with unsaved changes, the app asks first.

## 3. Entering a CR (නව අපරාධ වාර්තා / සංස්කරණය)

1. The top box (pink) has the office, year, CR number (just the number, e.g. `12`; it becomes `12/2026`), month and the half-year you are entering it in.
2. The sections of the old form are tabs: **Detection · Accused · Surety · Times · Cash · Seized items · Court**. Times are orange, cash blue and seized items purple, as before.
3. Dates are typed as **YYYY.MM.DD** (e.g. `2026.01.02`).
4. Click **Save**. If something is missing or wrong, the field is marked in red and its tab shows ⚠.
5. **Undo changes** puts back what was last saved.

To open an existing CR, use **Open an existing CR** at the top: office, year, number → **Open**.

- **Pictures:** after saving, **Add picture** below the tabs. Pictures and PDFs only.
- **Delete:** a deleted CR can be restored (**Restore**) later. Its number can be used again.
- **Someone else changed this record:** another user saved the same CR while you had it open. Note your changes, reopen the CR and enter them again.

## 4. Case progress (නඩු පවරන ලද අපරාධ වාර්තා)

Open the CR (office + number), then fill in:

- court, case number, court date, prosecuting officer and the case type;
- summons and warrant dates with their memos, and up to 21 trial dates (**Add hearing date**);
- the result grid: tick every result that applies. Fine and imprisonment open their own boxes (amount in rupees; months and days);
- date convicted, receipt, **add to the convicts register**, reward voucher;
- register references and who took over the CR file.

## 5. Viewing a CR (අපරාධ වාර්තාව සොයා බැලීම)

Shows every field of the CR and its case, read-only. **Print** prints it. Station heads and detecting officers also see **Edit**, **Case progress** and **ලේඛන සාදන්න**.

Click a row in any result to open that CR.

## 7. Reports (වාර්තා)

1. Choose the period: **මාසික** (monthly), **අඩවසර** (half-year) or **වාර්ෂික** (yearly).
2. Choose all CRs, **by detecting officer** or **by informant** (and the officer).
3. Choose the office, year and month or half-year once.
4. Click the report: register, warrant, trial, summons, not yet filed, fines not collected, reward vouchers due, resolved, fines earned, convicts register.
5. The result shows totals at the bottom. **Export to Excel**, **Export to CSV**, **Print**, **Save as PDF** or **Open in Word**.

**Summary** gives counts, quantities per item type and fines, by offence, detecting officer or informant. The three buttons below it open the case-number look-up, CRs by name/NIC and previous offences by court.

## 8. Making documents (ලේඛන සාදන්න)

1. Open the CR (CR view or entry) and click **ලේඛන සාදන්න**.
2. The templates for the CR's offence are listed by type. **Not yet confirmed by staff** means the department has not yet confirmed that this is the current version.
3. Tick **Also save as PDF** if you want a PDF too (needs Word on this PC).
4. Click **Make document**. Read any warning (for example, fields that were empty in the CR), then **Open** to edit or print it in Word, or **Show in folder**.
5. Documents made earlier for this CR are listed at the bottom.

The wording of the documents is never changed; only the fields are filled in from the CR.

## 9. Changing a printout in Word (Open in Word)

The registers, the convicts list and the previous-conviction certificate (E 48) have an **Open in Word** button next to **Print** and **Save as PDF**.

1. Click **Open in Word** (**Word හි විවෘත කරන්න**). The app saves the printout as a new Word file in `Documents\Excise documents\Print-outs` and opens it.
2. Change what you need (wording, widths, fonts, extra lines) and print from Word as usual.
3. Each click makes a new file (`… (2).docx`, `… (3).docx`); an earlier file is never overwritten.

An edited file is a copy: it does not change if the CR or the register changes later. Click **Open in Word** again to get a fresh one. Needs Microsoft Word on this PC.
